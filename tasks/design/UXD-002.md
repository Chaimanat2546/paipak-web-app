## Resulting flow

Detail → complete stay selections → Auth for Guest → price/availability revalidation → member booking review → payment action opens confirmation dialog → confirm to Payment step → verified booking/payment status. Member skips unnecessary Auth. Cancel Auth returns to its active origin; expired/reloaded intent is not recovered.

Review and confirmation are separate: the full review follows the inspected Airbnb information grouping, while the Desktop modal/Mobile bottom sheet before Payment follows the owner's Q-11. No Airbnb installment service, adults/children split, cancellation policy or property data is adopted.
